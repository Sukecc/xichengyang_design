Biography

A practice centered
on typographic voice.

Xicheng Yang is a typeface designer and researcher currently based in the UK. She graduated with distinction from the MA in Typeface Design at the University of Reading (2022), where she subsequently completed her doctorate in 2026. Her specialised research on the development of digital typefaces for the Tangut script—an extinct writing system used in northwestern China between the 11th and 13th centuries.

She redesigned Noto Serif Tangut for Google Fonts and engineered the custom typeface AraTangut for Professor Arakawa Shintarō. Her research has been presented globally at ATypI Stanford (2026), ATypI (2021), and the Chinese Embassy in the UK.
