# Xicheng Yang — Typeface Design & Research Archive

Welcome to the official repository of Xicheng Yang. This archive serves as a digital presentation of proprietary typeface systems, script revivals, and computational research utilities developed between 2018 and 2026. 

I am a typeface designer and researcher holding a PhD from the University of Reading (2026). My practice bridges systematic historical script analysis with contemporary digital font production, specialising in Chinese (Hanzi), Tangut, Latin scripts, and complex multi-script typography.

## 🏛️ Selected Project Overview

*   **Lyean (2022):** An award-winning expansive typeface family harmonising Latin, Arabic, and Simplified Chinese scripts under a unified typographic voice. Shortlisted for the Malee Scholarship.
*   **AraTangut (2023–Present):** A research-grade custom revival typeface designed for Professor Arakawa Shintarō, breaking new paradigms in digital typography for the extinct Tangut script.
*   **Shanghai Sky (2025):** Awarded the 70th New York TDC Award. A radical multi-script display system combining Kaishu, Running Script, and Small Seal Script textures across Hanzi, Latin, Manchu, and Japanese Kana.
*   **Noto Serif Tangut (2021):** A massive systemic redesign and technical overhaul of the official Google Fonts Tangut repertoire, encompassing 6,915 characters.
*   **Guqin Notation System (2018–2024):** A milestone non-codepoint variable synthesis system database generating over 200,000 reduced character forms following ancient musical notation norms.
