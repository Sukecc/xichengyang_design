Historical Reinterpretation via Variable Font Technology.

Developed as part of my PhD research, TangutBux is a typeface based on a historical woodblock print by an engraver whose family name was Bu (卜). Since the engraver's first name remains unknown, 'X' is often used to complete the name. It is an ongoing work-in-progress project focused on specialized research constraints.

Biography

A practice centred
on typographic voice.

I am a typeface designer and researcher currently based in the UK. I graduated with distinction from the MA in Typeface Design at the University of Reading (2022), where I subsequently completed my doctorate in 2026. My research focuses on the development of digital typefaces for the Tangut script—an extinct writing system used in northwestern China between the 11th and 13th centuries.

Throughout my practice, I have worked on various multi-script projects involving Latin, Chinese, and Japanese Kanji. My independent font design has won awards, including the New York TDC Award and recognition from the Tokyo TDC for AraTangut. My research has been presented globally at ATypI Stanford (2026), ATypI (2021), and the Chinese Embassy in the UK.
